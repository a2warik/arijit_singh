About this archive. This is a curated, actively-growing collection — not a literal claim of every one of Arijit Singh's 600+ recorded credits. It focuses on major Bollywood releases across his career, built to be easy to keep expanding. Every composer, lyricist, and vocalist is stored once and linked to their songs, so you can browse by name and see their full body of work with him. Spotted something missing or incorrect? That's the nature of a fan archive — it's never really finished.
